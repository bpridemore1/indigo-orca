1. What we do

We provide business formation support services for non-resident founders, including:

  • LLC formation ($297 + state fees): preparing and filing your Articles of Organization with the state you choose, registered-agent guidance, and EIN service.
  • EIN service ($69): preparing and filing Form SS-4 with the IRS as a foreign applicant, following up, and delivering your EIN.
  • Strategy call ($49): a 30-minute one-on-one consultation about formation, EIN, and banking.
  • Annual compliance ($149/year + state fees): reminders and filing assistance for your LLC's annual obligations.

2. What we don't do

We are not lawyers, and this is not legal advice. We prepare and file paperwork based on the information you give us. We do not advise on tax strategy, choice of entity beyond general information, immigration consequences, or whether a US LLC is right for your situation. If you need legal or tax advice, hire a licensed attorney or tax professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

3. What you do

  • Give us accurate, complete information. Filings are made on the basis of what you tell us — mistakes in your info are your responsibility to catch.
  • Respond to our questions promptly. Delays on your side delay your filing.
  • Pay state and government fees separately — they're set by the state/IRS, not us, and they change.
  • Use a valid email address — that's how we reach you.

4. Timing

Any timelines we mention are estimates, not guarantees. State offices and the IRS move at their own pace — especially for foreign applicants, where EIN processing can take several weeks. We file promptly on our side and keep you posted, but we can't control government processing times.

5. Money and refunds

  • Annual plan: cancel anytime; no partial-year refunds.
