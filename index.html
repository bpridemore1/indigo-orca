What the service does

Three things, done carefully. Most international founders need the first two; the rest are there when you want them.

LLC formation

We prepare and file your Articles of Organization, act as your organizer, and deliver your filed documents and an operating agreement. Wyoming is our standard recommendation for founders abroad — low state fees, no state income tax, and a registered agent is included in our price. If another state fits your situation better, we’ll tell you why on a strategy call before you pay for anything.

EIN filing

Your LLC needs an Employer Identification Number before it can open a U.S. bank account or sign up for payment processing — and without an SSN, you can’t get one online. We handle the paper filing with the IRS for you, or sell you the same step-by-step guide we follow, if you prefer to do it yourself and save the fee. Get the $29 DIY guide on Gumroad.

Strategy call

Thirty minutes with the founder, before you commit to anything. Which state, whether an LLC is even the right structure, what banking looks like from your country, what the first year actually costs. If the honest answer is “you don’t need us,” you’ll hear it on the call.

How it works

No account to create, no dashboard to learn. Email and documents, in this order.

1

Tell us about the business

A short intake: your name, your country, what the company will do, and the name you want. If anything about your situation is unusual, book the strategy call first — it’s cheaper than forming the wrong company.

2

We file with the state

We submit your Articles of Organization and, for Wyoming, set up your registered agent. You receive the filed documents and your operating agreement once the state confirms — typically a matter of days, depending on the state.

3

We file for your EIN

With the full-service option, we prepare and submit the no-SSN EIN application to the IRS and send you the confirmation when it arrives. This is the step that unlocks U.S. banking and payment accounts.

4

You open your accounts

With your filed articles and EIN in hand, you can apply for a U.S. business bank account and payment processing from home.

Questions founders ask

Do I need a Social Security number or a U.S. address?

No. A foreign founder can own a U.S. LLC without either. The EIN takes a paper filing instead of the online form — that’s the only real difference, and it’s the filing we handle.

Why Wyoming?

For most founders abroad it’s the simplest fit: a modest state filing fee, a low annual report fee, no state income tax, and no requirement to visit or live there. Our formation price includes the registered agent Wyoming requires. If your plans point to a different state, that’s a strategy-call question.

What do state fees add to the $259?

Only what the state itself charges to file — for Wyoming, the Articles of Organization filing fee. We tell you the exact figure before you pay anything; there is no markup on it.

Can the LLC open a U.S. bank account?

In most cases, yes — with your filed articles and EIN, several U.S. banks and financial platforms accept foreign-owned LLCs, and applications are generally handled remotely. We’ll point you to the realistic options for your country on a strategy call.
