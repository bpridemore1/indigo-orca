FAQ

Straight answers, no fine print.

Do I need a Social Security Number to get an EIN?

No. Non-resident founders can obtain an EIN without an SSN or ITIN by filing Form SS-4 as a foreign applicant. The $29 guide walks you through it yourself; the $69 service has us do it for you.

Which state should I form my LLC in?

It depends on your business. New Mexico and Wyoming are popular with international founders for low fees and privacy. Book a $49 strategy call and we'll talk through your specific situation before you file anything.

Can I open a US bank account as a non-resident?

Often yes, but the path varies by bank and your situation. Banking guidance is included with formation, and it's a core topic in the strategy call.

Is this legal advice?

No. Indigo Orca LLC provides business formation services, not legal advice. For legal questions about your specific situation, consult a licensed attorney.

Who actually does the work?

The founder, personally. No ticket queues, no outsourced processing lines. When you email, you get a human who knows your file.
